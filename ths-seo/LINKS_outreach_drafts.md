# Link outreach drafts for The House Supplier

Three templates for a person to adapt and send. Nothing has been sent. Replace every `[placeholder]`. Send from a company address, to the public business contact listed on the target's site, and keep each message to one request.

Before sending any of these, confirm how The House Supplier is related to Pacific Plumbing Supply and how that may be described in writing. The first and third templates depend on it.

---

## Notes for whoever sends these

- Do not offer payment, free product or a link in return for a link. A normal membership fee to a real association is fine; paying for the link itself is not.
- Do not ask for specific anchor text. The business name or URL is what these pages use.
- Navien and Resideo restrict online sales of their trade products. Read their current policy before asking either for a listing; a request could draw attention to listings they do not permit.
- Keep the business name, address and phone identical in every submission.
