# The House Supplier: backlink profile review

Data: Ahrefs Site Explorer, live links only, `thehousesupplier.com` with subdomains, pulled 2026-11-11. Nothing was changed in Ahrefs, Shopify or anywhere else.

## Verdict

- The profile is almost entirely automated spam from networks that sell SEO links. Ahrefs flags 1,666 of 1,676 live referring domains (99.4%) as spam.
- No editorial or industry link exists yet. The ten non-spam domains are five of our own redirecting domains, one sister store, and four automated store-profile sites.
- Most of the spam does not point at thehousesupplier.com. It points at five sibling domains that 301-redirect here, and arrives through those redirects.
- **Disavow: not recommended now.** Conditions that would change this are listed under "Disavow recommendation".

### Top referring domains by Domain Rating

Apart from scamadviser.com and brandfetch.com, every domain in the top 150 by DR is a link-seller domain. Examples: factmags.com (74), backlinker.shop (71), buybacklinks.agency (69), vividranklab.shop (59), strongboosthub.shop (59), seogrow.agency (58), seonix.agency (56), linkrankpro.shop (54), and about 130 `.store` domains at DR 48-49 named after "seoexpress", "link-baron", "outrank-hq" and "rank-forge".

The DR of these domains is inflated by the networks linking to each other. Nearly all of their links to us are nofollow, so they pass nothing, which is why our own DR stays at 0.7.

## Anchor text

Based on the 150 anchors with the most referring domains, which cover 4,460 of the 5,015 live links.

| Anchor group | Links | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Advertisements for SEO and link-selling services | 3,616 | 81.1% |
| Empty anchor (301 redirects from our own sibling domains) | 705 | 15.8% |
| Bare domain names (ours and the sibling domains) | 124 | 2.8% |
| Product names and other natural anchors | 13 | 0.3% |

- The single largest anchor (544 links from 356 domains) reads "Boost Rankings & Massive Traffic | High DR & High Traffic SEO Backlinks for Casino, Crypto For Competitive Nic...". This is the only place casino or crypto wording appears: it advertises the seller, it does not describe our store.
- No pharma anchors and no foreign-language anchors were found. All 200 sampled spam pages are in English.
- Typical anchors: "High Authority thehousesupplier.com Backlinks for Long Term SEO Ranking Growth" (510 links), "High Quality Dofollow Backlinks DA 50 PA 40 Premium PBN Network Service thehousesupplier.com..." (94 links), "Rank Higher on Google with thehvacsupplier.com Premium SEO Links and Backlinks" (268 links).
- The anchors name the sibling domains as often as the main domain: thehvacsupplier.com, thefastsupplier.com, thesupplierhouse.com, thesprinklersupplier.com, theinstallersupplier.com.
- 2,413 of the 4,460 links are dofollow. 705 of those are our own redirects; the remaining 1,708 or so are spam, mostly from very low-DR domains (DR 0-5).

The pattern (anchors that advertise the seller's own services with the victim's domain inserted, thousands of throwaway domains, several unrelated domains hit by the same templates) is the mass "SEO offer" spam that targets newly registered domains. It does not look like a campaign bought for this store, and it does not look like a targeted negative-SEO attack. One thing a person should confirm: that nobody on the team ordered a cheap backlink package (Fiverr or similar) for any of the six domains. Two of the spam domains have "fiverr" in their names.

## Disavow recommendation

**Do not submit a disavow file now.**

Reasons:

1. Google's stated position is that it ignores spam links of this kind, and that disavow is for sites with a manual action for unnatural links, or with a history of paid links that is likely to cause one. Neither is evidenced here.
2. The links are not the kind that earn a manual action for the receiving site: they are overwhelmingly nofollow, on throwaway domains, with anchors that promote the seller, not our keywords.
3. A disavow file here would have to list about 1,666 domains that will churn, and would need constant upkeep for no measurable gain.
4. The low DR is not caused by the spam. It reflects the absence of real links, which is what `LINKS_targets.csv` addresses.

Check first, since I could not see Search Console:

- Search Console > Security & Manual Actions > Manual actions, for thehousesupplier.com. If it shows "No issues detected", nothing more is needed.

Reconsider a disavow only if one of these happens:

- A manual action for "Unnatural links to your site" appears in Search Console.
- It turns out someone did buy links for one of the six domains.
- A new wave arrives with dofollow, keyword-rich commercial anchors (for example "buy Rheem water heater") from real-looking sites. That would look like manipulation on our behalf; the current anchors do not.

If a disavow ever becomes necessary, use `domain:` lines taken from a full Ahrefs export filtered on the spam flag, never a hand-picked list, and keep the ten non-spam domains out of it.

## Things for a person to decide (no change made)

1. **Sibling-domain redirects.** Five domains redirect every URL to thehousesupplier.com and carry roughly 86% of the spam with them. If any of these domains is not needed for customers or brand protection, removing its redirect would detach that share of the spam without a disavow. This is optional, not urgent, and belongs to whoever owns the domain strategy.
2. **Redirected junk URLs.** thefastsupplier.com and thesupplierhouse.com redirect paths that have nothing to do with this store, for example `/product-category/musical-instruments/synthesizers` and `/brand/canon`, to the same path on thehousesupplier.com. Those almost certainly end in 404 pages, and they suggest these domains carried, or were spammed with, an unrelated store's URLs. Worth a look by whoever handles the technical side.
3. **Sister-store link.** The thefirealarmsupplier.com link is legitimate, but it sits on thousands of paginated collection pages. One link in the footer or on an "our stores" page does the same job.

## Limits of this review

- Ahrefs data only. Search Console links report and manual-action status were not available to me.
- Anchor figures cover the top 150 anchors (4,460 of 5,015 links); the route figures come from a 200-domain sample.
- I did not open the spam pages themselves.
