Ruud is a brand of Rheem Manufacturing, and many Ruud tank water heaters are built alongside a Rheem model with a matching model number. The pattern is simple: the Ruud number carries RU where the Rheem number carries RH, so a Ruud PRO+G50-40N-RU62 lines up with a Rheem PRO+G50-40N-RH62. The series names differ as well: Ruud Professional Achiever Plus corresponds to Rheem Professional Classic Plus. This page lists the Ruud tank water heaters we carry and the Rheem model each one corresponds to, limited to pairs where published specifications for both brands show the same capacity, input and dimensions. Use it when a quote, a permit or an old data plate names one brand and you are looking at the other.

How to choose

  • Read the model number, not the brand. In a number such as PRO+G50-40N-RU62, G is gas (E is electric), 50 is the nominal gallon capacity, 40N is 40,000 BTU/h natural gas (P is propane) and RU marks the Ruud brand. An S after the capacity is a short tank; M2 on an electric model is a medium-height, two-element tank.
  • Match the series tier. PRO+ on both brands is the Plus tier: Achiever Plus for Ruud, Classic Plus for Rheem. A Rheem model beginning PROG without the plus sign is the Professional Classic tier and is not the same model, even when capacity and input match.
  • Confirm the fit by dimensions. For a replacement, compare tank height, diameter and vent size on the specification sheet with the space and vent you have. Equivalent pairs share these figures.
  • Size the same way for either brand. Rheem’s guidance is to estimate the hot water used in your household’s busiest hour and choose a first-hour rating that meets it.
  • Check warranty and registration by brand. Each brand publishes its own warranty terms. Read the terms for the label on the heater you buy.

Common questions

Are Ruud and Rheem water heaters the same?

Ruud is a Rheem Manufacturing brand, and the paired models on this page have the same published capacity, gas input or wattage and dimensions. The brand label, series name and model suffix differ.

What is the Rheem equivalent of the Ruud PRO+G50-40N-RU62?

The Rheem PRO+G50-40N-RH62, a 50 gallon tall, 40,000 BTU/h natural gas atmospheric vent water heater in the Professional Classic Plus series.

Is Ruud Professional Achiever the same as Rheem Professional Classic?

They are the corresponding series in each brand’s range: Achiever with Classic, and Achiever Plus with Classic Plus. Always confirm the individual model pair, because not every model is offered under both labels.

Can I use Rheem parts on a Ruud water heater?

Replacement parts are specified by model and serial number, not by brand name. Look up the part number for the exact model on the data plate before ordering.
