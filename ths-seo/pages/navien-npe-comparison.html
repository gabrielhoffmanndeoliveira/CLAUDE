How to choose

Navien’s sizing method is to work from the coldest day of the year, not the average one.

  • Work out your temperature rise. Subtract the coldest incoming water temperature from your set temperature. A 120°F setting with 50°F incoming water is a 70°F rise, so read the 67°F column and allow a small margin.
  • Add up peak demand. Total the flow of the fixtures that will run at the same time, then pick a model whose flow at your rise is equal to or greater than that total.
  • Compare sizes at the same rise. At a 67°F rise the NPE-150S2 delivers 3.4 GPM, the NPE-180 models 4.3 GPM, the NPE-210 models 5.1 to 5.3 GPM and the NPE-240 models 5.6 to 5.8 GPM.
  • A2 or S2. Choose the NPE-A2 when you want recirculation without adding an external pump and buffer tank. Choose the NPE-S2 when the home has no recirculation loop or an external one is already planned. Navien notes that energy use increases when a unit is set up for recirculation.
  • Gas line and vent. Navien states the NPE-2 series can run on 1/2" gas pipe up to 24 feet and can vent in 2" pipe up to 75 feet with a maximum of six elbows, deducting 8 feet per 90° elbow. Longer runs need larger pipe; confirm against the installation manual and the fuel gas code tables.
  • Condensing or non-condensing. NPE-2 models are rated 0.93 to 0.96 UEF and accept 2" or 3" PVC, CPVC or approved polypropylene vent. NHW and NPN models are rated 0.81 UEF and have their own venting requirements, so check the manual before pricing a vent run.

Common questions

What is the difference between the Navien NPE-A2 and NPE-S2?

Both use the same gas input in each size. The NPE-A2 includes the ComfortFlow system, a built-in buffer tank and recirculation pump. The NPE-S2 has neither. The A2 units are also a few pounds heavier: 77 lbs for the NPE-240A2 against 73 lbs for the NPE-240S2.

Is the NPE-240A2 more powerful than the NPE-210A2?

Yes. The NPE-240A2 has a maximum input of 199,900 BTU/h and the NPE-210A2 has 180,000 BTU/h. At a 45°F rise that is 8.7 GPM against 7.8 GPM. Both share the same cabinet dimensions.

Can a Navien NPE-2 use an existing 1/2" gas line?

Navien states that 1/2" gas pipe is supported up to 24 feet. Whether an existing line is adequate depends on its length, the other appliances on it and the supply pressure, so it must be checked against the gas pipe sizing tables.

Do the NHW and NPN models use the same venting as the NPE?

No. The NPE-2 is a condensing unit that vents in plastic pipe. The NHW and NPN are non-condensing units with hotter exhaust and different vent materials. Use the venting section of each model’s installation manual.
