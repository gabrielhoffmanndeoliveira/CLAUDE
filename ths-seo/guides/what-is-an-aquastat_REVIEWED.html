An aquastat is a switch that senses water temperature and uses it to control a boiler. Its sensing bulb sits in the boiler water, usually inside an immersion well, and its contacts turn the burner off at a high limit, keep the water above a low limit, or hold the circulator off until the water is hot. On most hot water boilers it is the gray box on the jacket with a temperature dial inside. This guide explains what an aquastat does, the main types, typical settings and how to choose a replacement.

What does an aquastat do on a boiler?

Depending on the model, an aquastat performs one, two or three functions:

  • High limit: opens the burner circuit when the boiler water reaches the set temperature. This is the operating limit that keeps the boiler from overheating during a long call for heat.
  • Low limit: fires the burner to keep the boiler at a minimum temperature when no thermostat is calling. It is used on boilers with a tankless coil for domestic hot water.
  • Circulator control: stops the circulator when the boiler water falls below the low limit, so the heat goes to domestic hot water first.

A room thermostat senses air and asks for heat. The aquastat senses water and decides whether the burner may fire and the circulator may run.

What is the differential?

The differential is the temperature change between the switch opening and closing again. For example, with a high limit set at 180°F and a 15°F differential, the burner shuts off at 180°F and can fire again when the water falls to about 165°F. A wider differential gives longer, less frequent burner cycles. Some controls have a fixed differential and others are adjustable.

Single-function aquastats (L4006, L6006)

Two terms on the label describe the switch action. Breaks on rise means the contacts open as temperature rises, which is how a high limit works. The Resideo L4006A1017/U breaks on rise, with a 100 to 240°F range and a 5 to 30°F adjustable differential. Makes on rise means the contacts close as temperature rises, used to start a circulator only when the water is hot. L6006 models have SPDT contacts and can be wired for either action.

Manual reset high limits (L4006E)

A manual reset aquastat is a safety backup. If the water reaches its setting, it opens the burner circuit and stays open until someone presses the reset button. The Resideo L4006E1000/U has a 130 to 270°F range and is supplied with a 1/2 in. well. A manual reset limit that trips means the boiler overheated. Find the cause before the boiler goes back into service.

Aquastat relays (L8148)

An aquastat relay combines a high limit with a transformer and relay. The room thermostat connects to the T T terminals, and the control's relay switches the burner circuit and the circulator. The L8148A1017/U has a 140 to 240°F range with a fixed 8°F differential, and the L8148E1265/U has a 180 to 240°F range with a fixed 15°F differential. These are common on gas boilers.

Triple aquastats (L8124) and electronic aquastats (L7224U)

A triple aquastat adds a low limit to the high limit and circulator functions. It is the usual control on an oil boiler with a tankless coil, which stays hot all year. Resideo's L8124 family is the common example. The Resideo L7224U1002/U is a universal electronic replacement for oil boiler aquastats with high limit, low limit and circulator functions and an LED display.

What should a boiler aquastat be set to?

Use the settings in the boiler manufacturer's manual. As general guidance:

  • High limit: fin-tube baseboard and radiators are designed for hotter water than radiant floors. Do not set the limit above the boiler's rated maximum. Radiant floors need much cooler water and get it from a mixing device, not from turning the boiler limit down. See the mixing valve guide.
  • Low limit: on a triple aquastat, Honeywell's instructions call for the high limit to be set at least 20°F above the low limit. A boiler without a tankless coil may not need a low limit at all; ask a technician whether it applies to your boiler, because some cast iron boilers have a minimum return water temperature.
  • Differential: a larger differential reduces short cycling.

The aquastat high limit is not the only protection. The boiler also has a pressure relief valve, and many have a separate manual reset limit or low water cutoff. None of these should be disconnected or set beyond the boiler's ratings.

Boiler will not fire: is it the aquastat?

  1. Power and thermostat. Check that the service switch is on, the breaker is closed and the thermostat is calling for heat.
  2. Manual reset. Look for a tripped reset button on a manual reset limit or, on an oil burner, on the primary control. Press it once only. If it trips again, stop and call a technician. Repeated resets on an oil burner can flood the chamber with unburned oil.
  3. Settings. A high limit set below the current water temperature keeps the burner off.
  4. Burner fires but the radiators are cold. Look at the circulator and at air in the system instead. See choosing a boiler circulator and how to bleed a radiator.

Testing the relay, transformer and contacts means working on live 120 V terminals and is technician work.

How do you choose a replacement aquastat?

Match the control being removed, using its full model number:

  • Function: single limit, aquastat relay, triple or electronic.
  • Switch action: breaks on rise, makes on rise or SPDT.
  • Range and differential: the same or wider range, and a fixed or adjustable differential as on the original.
  • Reset: automatic or manual. Never replace a manual reset limit with an automatic one.
  • Mounting: immersion well, strap-on or remote bulb. If the old well stays, check its thread size and insertion length. Replacement wells include the Resideo 121371A/U (1/2 in. NPT, 3 in. insertion). Changing a well means draining the boiler below that tapping.
  • Thermostat circuit: 24 V, millivolt or line voltage.

Coat the sensing bulb with heat conductive grease where the control's instructions call for it, so the bulb reads the water temperature accurately. Browse all aquastats and wells.

When to call a professional

  • Replacing an aquastat or aquastat relay. These controls switch 120 V and are part of the boiler's safety chain, and a wiring error can leave the burner without a working limit.
  • A manual reset limit or burner primary control that trips more than once.
  • A relief valve that discharges, or boiler water that is hotter than the limit setting.
  • Any change of control type, for example fitting a triple aquastat where a relay was used.

Before removing any cover, turn off the boiler service switch and confirm the power is off. Never jumper a limit control to get a boiler running.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between an aquastat and a thermostat?

A thermostat senses room air temperature and calls for heat. An aquastat senses boiler water temperature and controls the burner and circulator.

Can I replace an L8148 with an L8124?

Not directly. They have different functions and wiring. Replace with the same type unless a technician confirms the system needs a low limit.
